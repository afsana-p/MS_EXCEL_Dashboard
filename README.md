# MS_EXCEL_Dashboard
An end-to-end data analysis project in Microsoft Excel that turns a year of e-commerce sales data into an interactive annual report for **Vrinda Store**, an online fashion retailer selling ethnic and western wear.

An Excel dashboard analyzing 31,047 online fashion orders (2022) from Vrinda Store to understand customer behavior and find ways to grow sales.

## Key Insights
- Women generate about 64% of total revenue
- Top states: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh
- Adults (30-49 yrs) are the largest buying group
- Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart are the top sales channels
- March had the highest sales and orders

## Recommendation
Target women aged 30-49 in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh with offers on Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart.

Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Formulas
